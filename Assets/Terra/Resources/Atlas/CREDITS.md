# MyTerrain native atlas assets

Earth: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, July 2004, topography and bathymetry. Reto Stöckli / NASA Earth Observatory. Original 5400×2700 JPEG; imported at up to 4096 pixels and colour adapted by the native globe shader. Not live imagery. NASA imagery is generally not copyrighted in the United States; no endorsement is implied.

- Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/
- Original: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/july/world.topo.bathy.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg
- Media guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The globe mesh, shaders, graticule and UI shapes are original runtime code. Typography uses the project's existing TextMeshPro Liberation Sans asset and its retained license. No generated artwork, paid assets or runtime CDN.

The water texture remains MatrixRex / Uber Stylized Water, MIT. The original texture and notice remain in the landscape assets; an exported copy of the notice is retained in WebGLTemplates/Terra/licenses/WATER-LICENSE.txt.

Display type: **Lilita One**, Juan Montoreano, copyright 2011. SIL Open Font License 1.1; unmodified source from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lilitaone. Full license retained in `Fonts/OFL.txt` and the exported `licenses/LILITA-OFL.txt`. The native SDF asset is generated from that font for game titles and destination labels.

Web startup artwork: `Assets/WebGLTemplates/Terra/loading-landscape.webp` is the MyTerrain / “Shape your world.” illustration supplied by the user on October 25, 2026, specifically for the loading screen. It is exported at its original 1536×1024 size as WebP (quality 88), with no generated additions or changed composition. This is loading artwork, not evidence of current gameplay scenery or measured geography. It replaces the earlier native-globe crop; the native globe itself and its font/assets above are unchanged.
