The original study individually downloaded Wheat_3.fbx, Wheat_4.fbx and Wheat_Crop.fbx. The attempted full folder archive was not confirmed on disk and is not claimed as acquired.

Terra uses the mature Wheat_4 mesh in a three-stalk combined cluster with exaggerated proportions and a shared URP material. The earlier growth and harvested variants are evaluation sources, not new farming mechanics. Decorative crop scale and arrangement do not represent measured crops or yields.

Study 13: mature wheat combined into broader three-stalk groups, with a custom matte lighting shader and denser illustrative placement. No authored bitmap assets were generated.

Study63 adds the creator's original Lettuce_4.fbx (CC0, downloaded October25,2026). Exact download URL/hash/license and the preceding crop-form study are in `docs/evidence/phase2a/crop-forms-01/`. `CultivatedCropAssets.Build()` metric-bakes that source to1,392/390/84-triangle LOD meshes with the native material color adapted to URP Simple Lit. The broad-leaf crop replaces the Wisconsin grain illustration; other locations retain their prior renderer. Plant scale, spacing, soil beds and species are authored illustrations, not observations. The soil texture reuses the previously attributed `JulioGround/EarthColor` resource. No purchase or generated image.

Study64 adapts the broad-leaf material to a brighter illustrative green; native geometry remains unchanged. Raised beds and underlying cultivation now share restrained15% JulioVII earth brushwork through TerraGround. See the study64 evidence for actual renders; this is not accepted scenery.
