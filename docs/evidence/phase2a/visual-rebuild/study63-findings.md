## Construction and integration

`LandscapeRenderer.CropBeds.cs` uses the existing continuous curved row coordinate, four-metre bed spacing,2.5m plant spacing and a0.62m illustrative ridge. The existing artist earth map supplies both the ground color and bed material. Original `Lettuce_4.fbx` from Quaternius Ultimate Crops is unchanged; native material color is adapted to matte URP Simple Lit. Native geometry is metric-baked into1,392/390/84-triangle levels by `CultivatedCropAssets.Build()`. [Import/bake receipt](study63-import.json). Source/license/hash are retained in the preceding crop study and the asset attribution file. No purchase or image generation.

Wisconsin uses the new construction for managed baselines, surrounding cultivated context, cultivation proposals and retained land around buildings. Other locations keep their prior construction. Context is partitioned into150m regions with unique quad-centre ownership; plant bounds stay within mapped visual crop cover and outside management sites. Managed plots clip soil and every conservative plant-bound corner to their polygon. Existing living-margin and rotation clipping supplies their exact cropped bands. The ground shader changes only Wisconsin's cultivated material, retaining the existing reversible surface masks. All species, sizes, spacing and ridges are authored illustrations, not observed crop identity, surveyed practices, source elevation changes or model inputs.

`NatureLodProfile` supplies explicit20/8-pixel detail thresholds and0.6-pixel culling for these low, broad plants. Projected detail uses the larger of footprint dimensions and height, while shadow/frustum margins retain actual height. Existing vegetation without a profile retains its original detail extent and cutoffs. `NatureSubmission` is unchanged. Derived bed meshes use the existing landscape ownership/teardown path; the soil is a shared resource material.
