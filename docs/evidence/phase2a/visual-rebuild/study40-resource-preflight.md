## AssetQuest free demo

The creator's [Stylized Forest Kit](https://assetquest.itch.io/stylized-forest-kit) offers a separate free demo. Downloaded through its visible **Download demo** control on October 25, 2026. The full pack is paid and was not acquired. The creator's forest diorama was inspected in Chrome: layered ground plants and varied tall branching forms are useful construction references, but marketing imagery is not evidence of suitability at Terra's scale or in its browser renderer.

Local archive: `Builds/ArtResearch/AssetQuestForestDemo/Forest_Kit_AssetQuest_Itch_FREE_DEMO.zip` (ignored research storage), 3,541,333 bytes, SHA-256 `6443999b45ec592a430ae340b52b480108ec76e4afd4ed9cbaa2e38c474c3c55`.

The archive's `Forest_Kit_License_DEMO.txt` explicitly grants CC0 for personal, educational and commercial use. Creator: Asset Quest, www.assetquest.dev. The readme specifies the TGA alpha channel for plant cutouts, double-sided plant rendering, metalness zero, a shared color palette for solid props, and optional subsurface treatment. A fair trial must follow these instructions rather than use default opaque imports.

The demo contains 17 FBX models: birch, pine, blackberry and raspberry bushes, forest grass, green patch, fern, nettle, woodruff, stump, five mushroom/conk groups, robin and wren. It also includes a TGA plant atlas and PNG color palette. This is an alternative woodland/understory family, not a complete rural ground/water/crop solution. Birds and additional authored locations are not proposed scope.

Original evaluation plan (now completed; see findings): preserve the current export; import this bounded sample outside Resources; configure materials according to the readme; test actual silhouettes at ordinary play distance before choosing assets. Compare an integrated patch of the existing Wisconsin landscape, with unchanged scientific cover/heights and annual state. Reject if it adds fine noise or loses form at play distance. Do not infer quality from a single isolated model or restrict the camera to conceal it.
