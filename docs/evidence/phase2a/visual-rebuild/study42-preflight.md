# Study42 — Quaternius Ultimate Stylized Nature family

The creator's [May 2022 pack page](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html) links a public Google Drive folder, lists CC0, and describes FBX/OBJ/glTF/Blend assets with texture/normal maps. The FBX and texture folders were downloaded through Chrome on October 25, 2026. [Archive identities](study42-acquisition.json) record the exact bytes and hashes; originals are retained under ignored `Builds/ArtResearch/QuaterniusUltimateNature/`.

The downloaded family contains 63 FBX models and 21 PNG textures. It includes five normal trees, five birch, five maple, five pine, five palm, ten dead trees, three grass meshes, bush sizes/flower variants, plant/flower groups and rocks. Nine models and five matching textures were imported outside Resources for evaluation: three grass forms, three bush forms and NormalTree 1/3/5. No palms or new location are proposed. Source pixels and mesh geometry are unchanged; Unity texture imports cap evaluated textures at 2048 with mipmaps. Bark normal is imported as a normal map. This download has no Unity demo or supplied Unity shader, so the material baseline is URP/Lit with source color/normal maps, low smoothness and double-sided surfaces.

The included license wording is retained; line endings and trailing whitespace are normalized in the evidence copy. Its header says “Ultimate Platformer Pack”, apparently a reused header, while its text grants CC0. The authoritative nature pack page independently identifies this exact download as CC0. Do not silently rewrite the source license header. Free only; no image generation or paid source edition.

Native model readback: grass forms are 0.39–0.51m tall and 58–516 triangles; the three bush forms span 1.47–2.44m height and 190–400 triangles. Bush origins lie above their lowest geometry and must be offset to avoid buried/floating placement. NormalTree 1/3/5 are 6.87/5.28/2.98m tall at imported world scale and 8520/7096/2182 triangles respectively. FBX meshes have a child import scale of100; use world bounds and full local transforms rather than assuming mesh-local bounds are metres.

This is an evaluation candidate, not accepted art. Inspect its broad extruded grass and bush masses in the same Wisconsin landscape before considering production use. Relevant patterns TN-02/09/10/12; the [persistent reference](../../../DESIGN_REFERENCE.md) remains the bar.
