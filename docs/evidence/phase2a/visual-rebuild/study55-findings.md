The West field contains 3,636 grass roots and 203 mature flower groups (flower meshes have two submeshes, so raw batch records count 406 flower transforms). Height/grouping/species are illustrative. The saved annual maturity remains the only growth input; plants do not change money, outcomes, land cover or the source terrain. Current study applies to Wisconsin restore-grass and stewardship-agreement; cover crop, rotation, wind, other regions and source grassland retain their current treatments.

[42 lifecycle/render checks](study55-verify.json) verify both restoration and four-year stewardship: queue/undo/redo, fixed grass positions across ages, increasing growth, roots inside the site, exact geometry/cash after JSON reload, mature geometry matching the intended preview, replacement-preview cancellation and unchanged live session/source-world JSON. Checks cover roots, not every blade vertex. [Compilation](study55-compile.json) is clean. Replay scripts and hashed evidence are in [the checkpoint](study55-checkpoint.json).

The result gives restoration more structure and a visible establishment sequence. It does not close the reference gap: nearby crop marks are still mechanical, grass still reads as repeated blades in close views, and forest/ground cohesion is unfinished. Chrome transformation, timing, full HUD and alternate-site comparison remain required. No visual dimension is raised to 4.5 and no public export is claimed here.
