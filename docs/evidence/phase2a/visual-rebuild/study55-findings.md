# Study 55 — planted restoration and annual establishment

The study54 Chrome proposal remained a flat green rectangle with fine scattered marks. TN-02/05/08 call for readable vegetation masses and a visible transformation. This candidate uses the existing free Quaternius grass/flower family as low sward, connected taller swaths and flowering pockets. No downloaded resource, generated image, species claim or annual-model change.

[Matched close control](study55-control-close.png) and [initial candidate](study55-candidate-close.png) show the difference. The initial transient experiment did not change the annual session; its full growth is a visual diagnostic, not an accepted mature project. The retained implementation follows real saved maturity, with stable planting positions and progressively taller/wider grass and more flowers. Preview shows intended growth with the existing proposed tint/label. The initial brown establishment tint looked scorched; Wisconsin restoration now uses muted young green instead. Suspended status remains gray.

Actual compiled Editor captures use isolated annual fixtures through the real `DisplayAnnual` path: [proposed](study55-annual-year0-close.png), [Year 1 / 33%](study55-annual-year1-close.png), [Year 2 / 67%](study55-annual-year2-close.png), [Year 3 / operating](study55-annual-year3-close.png), and [operating at normal camera](study55-annual-year3-normal.png). Overlay HUD is absent in these 1280×720 camera renders; labels use the normal world-per-pixel scale. They are not Chrome or whole-interface acceptance.

The West field contains 3,636 grass roots and 203 mature flower groups (flower meshes have two submeshes, so raw batch records count 406 flower transforms). Height/grouping/species are illustrative. The saved annual maturity remains the only growth input; plants do not change money, outcomes, land cover or the source terrain. Current study applies to Wisconsin restore-grass and stewardship-agreement; cover crop, rotation, wind, other regions and source grassland retain their current treatments.

[42 lifecycle/render checks](study55-verify.json) verify both restoration and four-year stewardship: queue/undo/redo, fixed grass positions across ages, increasing growth, roots inside the site, exact geometry/cash after JSON reload, mature geometry matching the intended preview, replacement-preview cancellation and unchanged live session/source-world JSON. Checks cover roots, not every blade vertex. [Compilation](study55-compile.json) is clean. Replay scripts and hashed evidence are in [the checkpoint](study55-checkpoint.json).

The result gives restoration more structure and a visible establishment sequence. It does not close the reference gap: nearby crop marks are still mechanical, grass still reads as repeated blades in close views, and forest/ground cohesion is unfinished. Chrome transformation, timing, full HUD and alternate-site comparison remain required. No visual dimension is raised to 4.5 and no public export is claimed here.
