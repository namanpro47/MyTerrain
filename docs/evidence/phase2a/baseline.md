## Five material gaps

1. **TN-01 / TN-03:** the valley occupies a small central region while large pale panels dominate its edges. Recompose the camera and HUD together.
2. **TN-02:** woodland reads as uniformly spaced small specimens. Create canopy masses and variation without changing the source land classes or terrain.
3. **TN-04:** text-only tool cards lack a connection to their landscape treatments. Introduce a consistent original miniature illustration system.
4. **TN-05 / CS-04:** preview mixes instructions, provenance, cost and consequence into a single paragraph. Give identity, exact deltas, rejection and action separate hierarchy.
5. **AX-01 / TN-06:** utilities are dispersed, controls are small, and help/sources lack clear focus behavior. Establish a compact guide and complete keyboard control states.

Only the existing one-location slice is in scope. These are starting observations, not acceptance scores. Final browser output, motion, adverse states, timings and source identity must be evaluated before the Phase 2A gate can pass.
